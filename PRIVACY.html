Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Social Media Autoposter is a self-hosted application. Your data stays on your own server.

Data Collection

This application collects only the data you provide directly:

  • Social media account tokens (stored locally on your server)
  • Post content you create (stored in a local database)
  • Scheduling preferences

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the server where you host this application. No data is sent to third-party services other than the social media platforms you choose to post to (TikTok, Reddit).

Third-Party Services

When you post content, it is sent to the respective platform's API (e.g., TikTok Content Posting API). Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties beyond the social media platforms you explicitly connect.

Contact

For questions, open an issue at GitHub Issues.
